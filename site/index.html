The problem

The session is right. The medium isn't.

You know the moment. Your client is close to something real, and a notification pulls them away. A chat window blinks. Someone glances at a second monitor. The thread breaks, and you spend the next ten minutes finding it again.

Video calls made coaching accessible to everyone, everywhere. But they also made it flat. Presence is hard to build through a screen, body language disappears into a rectangle, and the tools that make coaching powerful in a room (space, movement, metaphor, distance) simply don't translate.

In-person coaching solves that, but only for the clients who can reach you.

Aura Arbor gives you the depth of a room and the reach of a video call.

What makes it different

Presence you can feel. Depth you can't fake.

The space

A space that shapes the work

A calm studio for structured dialogue. A forest for grounding and emotional work. A view of the stars when someone is stuck inside an immediate concern and needs distance. Inside the headset there's no inbox and no second screen. The environment is part of the intervention.

The tools

Think with your hands, not just your words

A shared whiteboard for mapping themes. Objects your client can place, move and work with: a bridge, a backpack, a door. Set two of them at whatever distance feels true, then move one and watch what shifts.

The presence

A different kind of presence

Meeting as avatars shifts something in the dynamic. Without the intensity of face-to-face scrutiny, many clients feel freer to speak openly and get to what matters sooner. The connection is real. It simply works differently than it does on a screen.

How it works

Four steps from booking to breakthrough.

  1. Create your sessionSet it up in seconds and invite up to two clients. No software install for them to figure out alone.
  2. Share the session codeEach client gets a unique code and joins directly. That's the whole handoff.
  3. Meet in the space you choseStudio, forest, abstract space or cosmic room: whatever the session calls for.
  4. Guide with tools and objectsWhiteboard, objects and the room itself become part of how you work.

Why now

Immersive coaching isn't a someday technology.

Headsets have become lighter, cheaper and comfortable to wear for the length of a real session. A generation of clients has grown up at home in digital spaces. And coaching itself has largely moved online, so the question is no longer whether you coach through a screen, but how much the screen costs you.

We built Aura Arbor for coaches who'd rather shape what comes next than adapt to it later.

Built in VR today. Designed for the immersive devices of tomorrow.
