Coaching tools

Shape the conversation.

Structure ideas, capture insight and guide the session without ever breaking immersion.

Coaching conversations rarely move in straight lines. A client circles a theme three times before naming it. Something said in minute six turns out to matter in minute forty. Aura Arbor gives you tools to hold that complexity visibly, inside the session, without reaching for a notepad or dropping out of the space.

The shared whiteboard

Map themes as they emerge.

Sketch a system. Write the one word your client keeps returning to and leave it standing where you both can see it. You and your client work on it together in real time. It's a shared surface, not a coach's presentation screen.

Why it works in VR

You turn toward it and keep talking.

On a video call, switching to a screen-share tool means everyone stops being present and starts operating software. Here the whiteboard is simply in the room with you.
