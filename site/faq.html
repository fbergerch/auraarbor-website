FAQ

Questions before you step in.

Equipment, setup, session length, confidentiality and cost. The practical questions coaches ask before their first immersive session.

What equipment do I need?

A standalone VR headset for you, and one for each participating client. No PC, cables or external sensors required. If you're testing the waters, headsets can be rented affordably for a single session.

Do my clients need to know VR?

No. Most first-time users find their way without a guide. Clients join with a session code. That's the entire technical requirement on their side.

How long does setup take?

Creating a session takes seconds. For a client's first time, plan a short orientation at the start so they can get comfortable in the space before the coaching begins.

Does it work for online and hybrid practices?

Yes. Aura Arbor is built for coaches working with clients anywhere. If you can currently coach someone on a video call, you can coach them here.

Is it suitable for corporate and executive coaching?

Yes. Coaches use it for leadership development, career and role transitions, team dynamics and conflict work, as well as personal development.

What about confidentiality?

Sessions are private between coach and invited clients. Nothing is recorded unless you choose to. Full details are in our privacy policy.

How long should a session be?

Coaches in testing generally find 30 to 45 minutes works well for a full VR session, often shorter than a video call while covering as much ground.

Can I take notes during a session?

This is one of the most frequent requests from coaches, and it's actively in development. We're building for the coach who doesn't want to remove the headset mid-session.

What does it cost?

Early access is free. We'll share pricing with early access members well before anything changes, and you'll have input into how it's structured.

How do I get started?

Join the early access list. We onboard coaches personally, so you'll speak with a real person, not a sequence of automated emails.
